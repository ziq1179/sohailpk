Act as a world-class UI/UX designer and web developer. Create a modern, premium, e-commerce landing page in English for an artisanal gourmet food brand (specifically Creamed Honey with real fruit/berry infusions), inspired by high-end design studios like Art. Lebedev Studio.

### Brand Identity & Aesthetic
- Name: "Demilie Creamed Honey" (or customizable brand name)
- Style: Playful elegance, vibrant active geometry, clean typography, and tactile appetizing visuals.
- Color Palette: Warm honey gold/amber base, off-white, deep charcoal text, paired with vivid flavor accents (bright strawberry red, blueberry indigo, pistachio green, lemon yellow).
- Typography: Bold, modern sans-serif headers with clean, highly readable body typography. Accent text with custom script/lettering style.

### Page Sections & Structure

1. Hero Section:
   - Full-width hero banner with a high-impact headline: "Whipped to Perfection. Infused by Nature."
   - Subtitle explaining creamed honey (100% natural raw honey, finely crystallized into a silky, spreadable texture).
   - Dynamic product showcase showing a signature honey jar centered with colorful pattern overlays.
   - Primary Call to Action (CTA): "Shop Flavors" and Secondary CTA: "Discover Creamed Honey".

2. Interactive Product Grid / Catalog:
   - Grid layout showcasing creamed honey flavors (e.g., Wild Berry, Raspberry & Vanilla, Mango Passionfruit, Pistachio Cream, Pure Golden Raw).
   - Card Design: High-contrast flavor cards featuring jar visual, active geometric/illustrated patterns corresponding to each flavor, price, weight (e.g., 250g / 8.8 oz), and "Add to Cart" quick button.
   - Hover Effects: Smooth hover animation revealing texture closeup or flavor notes.

3. "What is Creamed Honey?" Feature Story / Visual Process:
   - Interactive or step-by-step section explaining the cold-whipping process.
   - Highlight key product properties: 100% Natural, Never Drips, Spreadable Like Butter, No Artificial Flavors.
   - Clean 4-column metric/icon bar: Pure Sourced Honey, Zero Added Sugar, Cold-Processed, Eco-Packaging.

4. Flavor Tasting Notes & Usage Inspiration:
   - Interactive tab bar or carousel showing creative ways to enjoy the honey (e.g., "Spread on Warm Toast," "Swirled into Yogurt," "Drizzled over Pancakes," "Eaten with a Spoon").
   - Vibrant food photography overlay cards.

5. Premium Gift Box & Bundles Showcase:
   - Highlighting hexagonal/frame presentation gift boxes for holiday sets or corporate gifting.
   - Includes "Build Your Own Tasting Box" feature layout.

6. Social Proof / Customer Reviews:
   - Clean testimonial cards with star ratings, customer quotes, and verified buyer badges.

7. Footer & Newsletter:
   - "Join the Hive" newsletter subscription box offering a 10% discount on the first order.
   - Quick navigation links, shipping info, FAQs, social links, and wholesale/vending inquiries.

### Technical & UX Guidelines
- Fully responsive layout (optimized for mobile, tablet, and desktop).
- Micro-interactions on buttons, smooth scroll anchors, and subtle floating animations for honey jars/patterns.
- E-commerce UI elements: Sticky navigation header with logo, search bar, currency converter, and slide-out cart drawer.
