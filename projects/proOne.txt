Act as a senior creative frontend developer and designer. Build a cinematic single-page narrative web application inspired by dark luxury editorial sites.

### Design System
- Theme: Deep midnight black/charcoal background (#0a0a0c), sharp white primary text (#f8f8f8), gold/bronze metallic accents (#d4af37, #c5a059), and subtle red status accents (#ef4444).
- Typography: Use Google Fonts - Cinzel or Cormorant Garamond for cinematic headers, and Inter/Plus Jakarta Sans for body text.
- Styling: Tailwind CSS. Include subtle borders (border-white/10), dark ambient radial gradients, and gold glow effects (shadow-[0_0_20px_rgba(212,175,55,0.15)]).

### Site Structure & Features
1. Navigation & Audio Bar:
   - Floating header with sound toggle (Web Audio API or ambient background loop), chapter index dropdown/modal, and progress indicator.

2. Hero Section:
   - Dramatic headline with metallic gradient text: "THE LAST DANCE FOR GLORY".
   - Subtitle: "A CINEMATIC PROPHECY · SUMMER MMXXVI".
   - Ambient countdown timer or interactive scroll call-to-action ("SCROLL TO BEGIN").

3. Narrative Chapters (Card & Grid Sections):
   - Chapter I: The Legends (Grid of player spotlight cards showing age/number, status badge e.g., 'ELIMINATED', quote/story, and bold typography).
   - Chapter II: The Contenders (Ranked list with status tags, match commentary, and highlight borders).
   - Chapter III: The Underdogs & Heirs (Responsive 2 to 3 column layout showcasing young talents and rising teams).
   - Chapter IV: Interactive Mountain / Bracket (Selectable nations filter that highlights their remaining path/stats).
   - Chapter V: The Storylines & Stats Counter (Grid of minimal stat counters e.g., 0 Hosts, 0 Cities, 0 Trophy, 1 Winner).

4. Animations & Interactivity:
   - Use Framer Motion for fade-in section reveals on scroll.
   - Smooth hover scale transitions on cards.
   - Interactive nation selection state that filters content in real time.
